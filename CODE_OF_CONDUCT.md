# Code of conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when discussing code, architecture, or migration
  risk.
- Treat uncertainty in legacy behavior as a shared investigation problem.
- Respect privacy and never post credentials or real personal/payment data.
- Credit prior work and preserve upstream attribution.
- Focus review comments on the contribution, not the contributor.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure
of sensitive information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainer may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.
